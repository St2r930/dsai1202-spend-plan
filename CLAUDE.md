# Project: Spend Plan

Single-page finance tracker for DSAI1202 (PolyU, Autumn 2026). Everything lives in `index.html`.

## AI work log rule

This is a university project (DSAI1202) graded on how we work with AI.
After every task where something notable happened, append an entry to
`docs/ai-log.md` using its template. Notable means: you made a mistake,
your first attempt was wrong, the user rejected or changed your
suggestion, something was hard, or you did something surprisingly fast.

- Fill in honestly: date, "Petr · Claude Code", what was asked,
  what you did, what went wrong or what had to be checked.
- Leave "What we decided and why" and "Lesson" as "(Petr to fill)".
  Never write lessons for the user.
- Include the commit hash if there is one.
- Do not log routine tasks that went smoothly.
