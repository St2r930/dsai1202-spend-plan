# Testing plan

Live site: https://st2r930.github.io/dsai1202-spend-plan/

Testing dates: **1 November – 6 November** (6 days).

## What to do

1. Open the live site. Click **Set up my budget** on the yellow banner at the top.
2. In **Setup**, set your budget period (start date, length) and enter your budget for that period in HK$. Add any bills due during the period (rent, transport, phone, etc.).
3. For 6 days, add every spending as it happens — food, fun, transport, everything.
4. Each day, write one line in our team chat or a notes app:
   - Did the daily limit feel realistic?
   - Did any verdict (Fine / OK / Think twice / Can't afford) feel wrong?
5. After 6 days, answer:
   - What was useful?
   - What was confusing?
   - What was missing?
6. Click **Export CSV** and send the file plus your daily notes and answers to Petr in the team chat by **6 November**.

## Note

All data stays only in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to any server.
